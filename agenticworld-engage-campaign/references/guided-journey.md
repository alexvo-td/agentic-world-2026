## Plot-twist default

1. **Start immediately.** Introduce the conference-list task and ask missing email/first/last together. With complete current-invocation identity, proceed to a new fixture. Recap handoff choices only if explicitly supplied; absence means a fresh workshop, not a history search.
2. **Inspect and add confirmation.** Verify list provenance, apply inherited criteria and check original-audience overlap where evaluable. Add the participant with the separate identity-only QA artifact under profile-contract.md; never change business scores to make them eligible.
3. **Show impact.** Display plot-twist.md's compact report: list rows, eligible/excluded profiles, known overlap/additional reach, observed signals and persona hypothesis, actual business delivery plus one QA destination. Missing overlap is unavailable, not zero. Do not repeat the full audience exercise unless this list changes a business decision.
4. **Resolve only relevant copy changes.** Reuse inherited copy when appropriate. If adjustment is needed, show current and proposed subject/opening before offering Keep current message (recommended when consistent) or Adjust opening for the conference list. Explain each effect and wait for the choice unless supplied/delegated. Conference references need source evidence. Keep the approved offer and fixed products; no invented urgency, prices, exclusivity or individual affinity.
5. **Preview and approve.** Apply decisions automatically and show what changed, full personalized email, actual business-versus-QA recipients and sender/settings. Retain the four-section review under launch-review.md. Once ready, show one Approve and launch this campaign action. Generic looks good is feedback, not launch authorization.
6. **Activate and observe.** Recheck the read-back fingerprint and launch the approved version once. Poll boundedly; submitted/processing/unknown do not mean delivered. Invite inbox verification. Generate performance automatically when already requested by participant/workshop brief; otherwise offer once. Show live logs, simulation limitations and fresh-query refresh behavior.
7. **Hand off to Journey.** Save decisions, snapshot/campaign/report references and observed outcomes in journey-handoff.md. Propose the next automation step; do not create a journey or recurring send at this stage.

## Shortcuts and recovery

- Use our selected audience and existing copy: keep both, show list impact, then prepare review without new choice questions.
- Use your recommendations: apply recommendations, show evidence/preview and retain final launch approval.
- Choice tool returns no answer: keep proposed content provisional and show a draft; silence is not approval.
- Prospect-only list lacks history: do not synthesize history/consent. Resolve intended acquisition strategy; the host supplies verified eligibility.
- Business audience or known additional reach is zero: show exclusions and retain a QA preview; no customer activation claim or business launch.
- CSV-only/add-row task: save/validate and stop; do not force campaign/reporting/journey stages.
- Technical failure: preserve artifacts/run, show a concise host-facing prerequisite and continue independent preparation. Do not ask marketers to choose SQL/runtime/IDs.
