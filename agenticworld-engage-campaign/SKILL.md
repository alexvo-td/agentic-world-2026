# Agentic World Engage Campaign

Default to the Plot-twist workshop stage: Northstar receives a conference list of existing customers with renewed contact and needs to activate it quickly. Carry forward Customer Discovery decisions only when explicitly supplied in this invocation; never search for them. Use the supplied Northstar template, fictional 20% offer and sofa/rug/lamp unless the inherited brief establishes different approved content. New prospects need separately defined eligibility and acquisition copy; never manufacture purchase history to make them overdue. Without an explicitly supplied discovery handoff, start a fresh Plot-twist workshop with a new fictional conference list. Do not investigate whether prior discovery exists. Support standalone win-back when explicitly requested.
